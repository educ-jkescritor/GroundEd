# Journey map: course instructor reviewing a module

> **Status: draft for review (Phase 2).** Primary persona: Prof. Ana Reyes ([personas.md](personas.md)).
> Emotions and pain points are assumptions to check in the usability test.

| Stage | 1 Notice the need | 2 Discover GroundEd | 3 Upload | 4 Wait | 5 Scan the report | 6 Check one finding | 7 Decide | 8 Share | 9 Return later |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Doing** | Term is starting or chair asks for a curriculum update | Gets a link from a colleague or a post | Uploads module PDF (or tries a sample) | Watches progress | Reads the headline + top findings | Opens a finding: quote ⇄ source | Update / Keep / Dismiss | Exports report for chair | Re-runs next term; checks history |
| **Thinking** | "Which of my tools are old? I have no time." | "Is this another AI tool that makes things up?" | "Is my file safe? Will it work on my PDF?" | "How long? Is it stuck?" | "What should I fix first?" | "Is this really in my module? Is the source credible?" | "I teach jQuery on purpose." | "Will my chair accept this?" | "What changed since last time?" |
| **Feeling** | Pressured | Sceptical | Cautious | Impatient | Curious → relieved | Critical | In control | Confident | Reassured |
| **Pain points** | Manual review takes days | AI tools without sources | Unclear privacy, file limits | Black-box waiting | Too many items, unclear order | Vague claims, broken links | Tools that auto-change content | Hard to show evidence | Starting from scratch |
| **Opportunities (design)** | Landing states the time saving | Example finding with real source on Landing; "no source, no claim" | Privacy note; clear scope (tech courses, text PDFs); samples | Named steps + estimate; can leave the page | Headline "N items may warrant review"; High first; filters | Side-by-side evidence; tier + date; page highlight | Three clear decisions + reason; "Keep intentionally" | Export with sources, decisions, "as of" date | History (demo account); same file → same result |

## Moments that make or break trust
1. **First finding they open** (stage 6): the quote must really be on that page, and the source link must work.
2. **First disagreement** (stage 7): "Keep intentionally" and "Dismiss" must be as easy as "Update".
3. **First AI text** (stage 7): must be visibly labelled and editable, never presented as fact.

## Student journey (short)
Find GroundEd → try with their lab guide → read results with **plain-language tooltips** ("What does end-of-life
mean?") → learn what to study on top. Same screens; no extra features, just clearer explanations.
