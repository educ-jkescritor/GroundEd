# Jobs to be done

> **Status: provisional (Phase 1).** Derived from the personas in [personas.md](personas.md).

A "job" is the progress a person is trying to make, written as:
**When** (situation) **I want to** (motivation) **so I can** (outcome).

## Main job (primary persona: course instructor)
**When** I prepare my module for next term,
**I want to** know which technologies and practices in it may be outdated, with proof,
**so I can** update what matters and still teach confidently, without spending days checking everything myself.

## Supporting jobs
| # | When… | I want to… | So I can… | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I open my results | see the most important items first | spend my limited time well | Instructor |
| 2 | an item is flagged | see the quote from my module and the source side by side | judge for myself whether it's right | Everyone |
| 3 | I disagree, or teach something old on purpose | mark it "keep intentionally" with a reason | the tool respects my decision | Instructor |
| 4 | I agree something is outdated | get a suggested replacement or draft wording | start the update faster | Instructor, designer |
| 5 | I've finished reviewing | export a report with sources and my decisions | share it with my chair or accreditor | Instructor, reviewer |
| 6 | I review someone else's module | get a quick overview and consistent results | compare and discuss fairly | Reviewer |
| 7 | I first hear about GroundEd | try it on a sample, without uploading or signing up | decide if it's worth my time | Everyone |
| 8 | a result seems wrong or unclear | understand how it was decided (sources, score rules) | trust it, or report the problem | Everyone |
| 9 | I'm studying from a tech module | check whether what it teaches is still current, in plain language | know what to learn on top of it | Student |
| 10 | I upload a module outside tech courses (e.g. GED) | be told clearly that it isn't covered yet | not get misleading results | Everyone |

## How the "Turnitin pattern" maps to these jobs
| Familiar step (originality checkers) | GroundEd equivalent | Jobs served |
|---|---|---|
| Upload a paper | Upload a module PDF (or pick a sample) | 7 |
| Wait while it's checked | Progress steps: reading → detecting → checking sources → scoring | — |
| Report with one headline summary | Headline: "N items may warrant review (H high · M medium · L low)" | 1, 6 |
| Document view with coloured highlights | Module pages with highlighted quotes, coloured by priority | 1, 2 |
| Side panel of matched sources | Side panel of findings, each with its trusted source | 2, 8 |
| Click a highlight → see the source | Click a highlight → finding detail: quote vs source, score breakdown, decision | 2, 3, 4 |
| Download report | Export report with evidence and decisions | 5 |

**Scope:** technology-related courses only; general education (GED) courses are not covered yet (job 10).

**Important difference:** originality checkers often *judge* a student. GroundEd *advises* a colleague.
So there's no blame language, no single "bad" percentage, and the faculty always has the last word.

## Success measures (to test in Phase 7)
| Job | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 7 Try it | Time from landing page to seeing the first finding (sample) | under 1 minute |
| 1 Priorities | Can name the top item to fix after looking at results | under 30 seconds |
| 2 Evidence | Can explain *why* an item is flagged using the evidence | under 30 seconds, no help |
| 3 Keep / 4 Update | Can record a decision on a finding | first try, no errors |
| 5 Export | Can produce a shareable report | under 1 minute |
| Trust | "I would trust these results" rating | at least 4 out of 5 on average |
